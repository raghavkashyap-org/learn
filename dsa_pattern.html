how to actually use this

Treat this as triage, not diagnosis. A trigger narrows you to a family of 1-3 techniques in seconds — that's the whole value. It cannot tell you which technique inside that family is correct; that still requires understanding the invariant each technique relies on (monotonicity for sliding window, the greedy-choice property for greedy, overlapping subproblems for DP).

The fastest way this table fails you: seeing "optimization problem" and reaching for DP by reflex when a two-line greedy argument would've solved it in O(n log n), or reaching for backtracking on a "how many ways" question that just wanted a formula. When a caveat is attached below, that's usually the exact spot people lose marks or minutes.
